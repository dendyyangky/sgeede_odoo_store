Color-Coded Notes

Choose from 4 colors: yellow, red, blue, and green to visually categorize your notes by urgency or type. Color is saved per note and always visible at a glance.

Always Above the Lines

Notes appear directly above the order lines, not buried at the bottom of the chatter. The moment you open a record, your notes are the first thing you see.

Collapse and Expand

Toggle the entire notes zone open or closed with one click. A badge shows the note count even when collapsed so you always know notes exist without the zone taking up screen space.

Author and Date Tracking

Every note automatically records who wrote it and when. Author name and creation date are displayed on each card with no manual logging needed.

Dark Mode Support

Fully compatible with Odoo's built-in dark mode. All note colors, the notes zone, and the add form adapt automatically with no extra configuration needed.

Sale, Purchase and Accounting

Works across all three core modules: Sale Orders, Purchase Orders, and Accounting entries including Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, and Credit Notes. One install covers your entire workflow.

Where do the Sticky Notes appear?

Sticky Notes are displayed directly above the order lines on Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, and Invoices, making them immediately visible when opening a record.

Who can see the notes?

Notes are visible to all users who have access to the document. Visibility follows standard Odoo access rights and permissions.

Are notes linked to the document permanently?

Yes. Each note is attached to its specific document and stays available throughout the entire workflow, from creation to completion.

Does it support dark mode?

Yes. The Sticky Notes interface automatically adapts to Odoo's dark mode, including colors and layout, with no extra setup required.
